## cachyos-settings is deliberately LEFT OUT of here: it provides/obsoletes
## zram-generator-defaults and dnf5 aborts 'dnf install' when both land in the
## same transaction ("conflicting requests"). The swap is done by the script
## itself, in phase 3 (the "cachy kernel glue" block).
